FAQ

Why can't I download from YouTube or Instagram?

Neither offers an approved way to save videos from a link. ClipSave shows what it can and won't bypass restrictions.

A download failed. What now?

Check that the link opens without signing in, then retry. Files over the size limit are refused.

How do I get a video into my phone's gallery?

Your browser saves it to Downloads or Files. Move it to Photos from there.

What do you store?

Links are processed in memory and not logged. Files stream straight to you and aren't kept.

Is this legal?

Only save videos you own or have permission to save. You're responsible for respecting copyright and platform terms.
